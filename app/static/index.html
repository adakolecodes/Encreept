Encreept

Let them read it. Stop them lifting it.

Encreept redraws every page of your document as a watermarked image, then locks the PDF. There's no text left to select, so nobody can copy a paragraph out of it.

What happens to your file

  1. Word and PowerPoint files are converted to PDF.
  2. Each page becomes an image stamped with the Encreept watermark.
  3. The new PDF is encrypted, with copying, printing and editing turned off.
  4. You download it, and the server deletes its working copies.
Page quality

What this can't stop. Anyone who can see a page can photograph it or run text recognition on a screenshot, and some PDF tools ignore lock settings. The watermark stays in those copies, and copying takes real effort. Keep your original: Encreept's PDFs can't be turned back into editable documents.
