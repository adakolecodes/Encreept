Encreept

Let them read it. Stop them lifting it.

Encreept redraws every page of your document as an image, then locks the PDF. There's no text left to select, so nobody can copy a paragraph out of it.

What happens to your file

  1. Word and PowerPoint files are converted to PDF.
  2. Each page becomes an image, along with any images you add, and gets your watermark if you choose one.
  3. The new PDF is encrypted, with copying, printing and editing turned off.
  4. You download it, and the server deletes its working copies.
What do you want to do?

Page numbers count your PDF's pages as they are now. If both images go in the same place, image 1 comes first.

Page quality
Up to 25 characters

What this can't stop. Anyone who can see a page can photograph it or run text recognition on a screenshot, and some PDF tools ignore lock settings. A watermark stays in those copies, and copying takes real effort. Keep your original: Encreept's PDFs can't be turned back into editable documents.
